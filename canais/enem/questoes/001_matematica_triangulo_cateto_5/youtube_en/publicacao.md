## Description
```
A right triangle has one leg equal to 5, and all three sides are whole numbers. Find the hypotenuse a and the other leg b.

In this lesson, step by step:
• The names: hypotenuse and legs
• The Pythagorean theorem (with the 3-4-5 example)
• Why "whole-number sides" is the key piece of information
• A special product: the product of a sum and a difference, (x + y)(x − y) = x² − y²
• Divisors of 25 and the system: a = 13 and b = 12
• The same method with a leg of 7 (the 7-24-25 triangle)
```
