## Description
```
The iPhone Duo, Apple's first foldable iPhone (from $1,999), hit stores on Oct 23, 2026 — and some owners report the inner screen cracking along the fold, sometimes within hours.

What we know:
• The reports are similar: you open or close the phone, hear a crunch, and a white line appears on the screen
• Apple confirmed an issue on "a small number" of units and says to contact AppleCare for a replacement; AppleCare+ isn't required and no recall has been announced
• iFixit tore it down: the breaks cluster near the hinge, where a support bar may be pressing on the screen — a theory, not yet confirmed
• Out of warranty, an inner-screen replacement costs $999 (Apple's price), half the phone's price
• Nobody knows yet how many phones are affected, and some images going around appear to be fake
• iFixit CEO Kyle Wiens: "A manufacturing variance or grain of sand can kill the fragile display panel it protects."

Phone, hinge and images in the video are illustrations. Narration uses a synthetic (AI) voice.
```
