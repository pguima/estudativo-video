## Descrição
```
A Xiaomi TV showed a sponsored ad before you could even open Netflix. Developer Gathin (@gathint) gave Claude Code the TV's model number: it found the TV on Wi-Fi, disabled 25 Xiaomi packages over ADB and wrote a new Apple TV-style launcher. The post hit 7.4M views and the open-source project, cleanyourtv, passed 27,000 GitHub stars in 6 days.

⚠️ Android TV / Google TV only. Apps are disabled, not uninstalled, and a restore script undoes it. Turn debugging off when you're done. At least one person reported bricking a TV, so try it at your own risk.
```
