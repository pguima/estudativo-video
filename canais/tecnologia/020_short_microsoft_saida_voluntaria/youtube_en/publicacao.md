Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees — almost a quarter of the company.

What we know:
• It covers the U.S., Canada, India and the U.K., and goes up to vice president (some teams are excluded)
• The package, according to Business Insider: 1 or 2 weeks of pay for every 6 months on the job, plus stock for 6 to 12 months and health care for a while
• The reason: the cost of AI. Microsoft plans to spend over $170 billion in 2026, largely on AI data centers
• Back in May, it made a similar offer to about 9,000 people
• Employees have until early December to decide
• Chief people officer Amy Coleman wrote in the memo: "The Voluntary Exit Offer is new for us. We'll learn from it."
