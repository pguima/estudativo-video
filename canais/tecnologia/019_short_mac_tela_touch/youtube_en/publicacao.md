In 2010, Steve Jobs said touchscreen laptops don't work: "your arm wants to fall off." 16 years later, Apple just launched the first touchscreen Mac.

What we know:
• It's the new MacBook Pro with an OLED (PROLED XDR) display, 14- and 16-inch, with M5 Pro and M5 Max chips
• You can scroll with your finger, pinch to zoom, move windows and even draw right on the screen
• The iPhone's Dynamic Island comes to the Mac, at the top of the screen
• The keyboard and trackpad stay; per MacRumors, touch can be turned off
• Starts at $2,899 (14"), $400 more than the comparable non-touch model, which is still on sale
