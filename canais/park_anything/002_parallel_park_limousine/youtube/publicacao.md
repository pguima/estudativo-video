Sources:
- California DMV, Driver's Handbook – Parallel parking: a space at least 3 feet longer than your vehicle, about 2 feet from the car next to you, finish within 18 inches of the curb.
- Manitoba Public Insurance, Driver's Handbook – Parallel parking: line up the rear bumpers, back in at 45°, straighten, then turn the other way.
- Limousine model: Lincoln Town Car (117.7-inch wheelbase) with a 120-inch stretch, 28 feet long.
The limo method, the 20° angle and the ~40-foot space come from our own simulation of that limo, not from an official manual. Real limos vary; always follow your training.
