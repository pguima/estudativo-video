In this simulation, Nvidia falls twenty-one percent by lunch. Close to a trillion dollars, gone.

That sounds impossible, until you remember the real record. In January 2025, Nvidia lost five hundred eighty-nine billion dollars in one day.

So why would it pop? Picture a circle. A chip maker invests in an AI lab. The lab rents computing power. That money comes back around to buy more chips.

It works as long as real customers eventually pay enough to justify it. Critics have warned about these circular deals for more than a year.

In our scenario, three of the biggest buyers quietly cut their spending plans on the same morning. The circle stops.

At school, Leo's phone buzzes in third period. His portfolio is already down thirty-one percent.

Here is what he misses. The S&P 500 sits inside millions of retirement accounts, and its ten biggest companies make up nearly forty percent of it.
