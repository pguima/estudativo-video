## Descrição (inglês, principal)
Every headline has a next 30 days. We live them first.

Survive the Headline is an animated channel where one ordinary family, the Riveras, lives through the crises everyone is talking about: an AI crash, a two-week blackout, a collapsing dollar, a heat dome.

Hour by hour and day by day, a live survival panel tracks their money, food, power and stress. Dan, Mia, Leo, Bea and Biscuit the dog come back in every episode.

Halfway through, you choose what you would do. At the end, the family gets a survival score, and you get three things you can do today.

These are simulations, not predictions. The family is fictional, the data is real, and the sources are listed in every video description. Nothing here is financial, medical or legal advice.

New episode every week. Comment A or B, and help decide what the Riveras do next.
